# Entry prompt

Bounty: `u-boothlord-unsent-the-micro-confession-sprint-8c6596ba` — "Unsent:
The Micro-Confession Sprint", $2, deadline 26 Nov 2026 08:30 WIB, blind
rounds x3 (16:30 WIB 25 Nov / 00:30 WIB 26 Nov / 08:30 WIB 26 Nov), 812
submissions, FREE (entryFeeUsd null).

Write one micro-confession in exactly two parts: a To: line naming who it
was meant for (a role, a first name or a relationship — no real full names
or handles) and the unsent message itself, one line written exactly as it
was typed, with no explanation after it. `main.ts` writes it to
`/output/entry.txt` (1–260 characters total; message line at most 200
characters). Aim to WIN, not just pass checks: the judge picks the entry
that feels most genuinely unsent — specific and honest, with a felt reason
it stayed in drafts, where the To: line and the message need each other.
Pure jokes, greetings, motivational lines and anything that reads as
written to be sent lose.
